📖 How to Set Up & Share a Trip

Only one person (the trip organizer) needs to do Steps 1–3. Friends just click your Share Link!

Step 1: Create a Blank Google Sheet

Open sheets.new on your computer and name it after your trip (e.g. 2026 Japan Trip).

Step 2: Paste the Backend Script

In your Google Sheet menu, click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete everything inside Code.gs, paste the copied code, and click the Save (💾) icon.

Step 3: Deploy as Web App
  1. Click Deploy → New deployment (top right of Apps Script).
  2. Click the gear icon ⚙️ next to Select type and choose Web app.
  3. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  4. Click Deploy → Authorize access (Advanced → Go to Untitled project → Allow) → Copy the Web app URL.
Step 4: Connect & Share With Friends

Click + Trip in the top bar of this page, paste your Web App URL, and configure your trip in Trip Setup & FX. Then click Share Link to send the magic link to your group chat—no setup needed for them!
